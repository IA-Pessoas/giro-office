# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People in an organization use Giro Office to run office operations through modules that match their work and permission level. RH administrators manage employee records and recurring people workflows; employees and viewers consult information they are authorized to see. Super admins operate the platform across organizations.

## Product Purpose

Giro Office brings operational work for multi-organization offices into one web application. It provides clear, dependable workflows while keeping each organization's data and each user's access scoped to their role.

## Brand Personality

Clear, dependable, and approachable. The existing Office RH interface is the visual and interaction reference for CCE; the workflow should feel familiar to people already using the module.

## Anti-references

Avoid disconnected mini-apps inside the Office, decorative dashboard patterns that compete with the task, and legacy behavior that weakens organization boundaries or server-side authorization.

## Design Principles

- Keep work in the RH context and reuse established Office components.
- Make organization scope and permission boundaries hold on the server.
- Show the state of a workflow clearly, including loading, empty, error, and completed states.
- Keep common actions direct and preserve existing records when a workflow is updated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible labels, keyboard-accessible interactions, clear focus states, and readable contrast consistent with WCAG AA. Do not rely on color alone to communicate evaluation state.
